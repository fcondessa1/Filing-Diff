# What has Apple said about tariffs over the last two years?

AAPL · 12 passages · model `claude-sonnet-5-5` · coverage: **answered**

Starting with the 10-Q filed 2025-05-02, the company said new U.S. tariffs were announced beginning in the second quarter of 2025, including on imports from China, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam and the EU, and that several countries imposed or threatened reciprocal tariffs. Across all later filings it warned that tariffs can have a material adverse impact on its business, supply chain, pricing and gross margin, and that the ultimate impact remains uncertain. By the 10-Q filed 2026-01-30 it noted that the Section 232 semiconductor investigation's initial results (January 14, 2026) did not impose additional tariffs affecting its products. The 10-Q filed 2026-05-01 added that the Supreme Court struck down certain IEEPA tariffs on February 20, 2026 and that the company was applying for a refund, and the 10-Q filed 2026-07-31 said it has applied for refunds, recognizes any received as a reduction of products cost of sales, and mentioned recent Section 301 tariffs.
