# What has Apple said about tariffs over the last two years?

AAPL · 12 passages · model `claude-sonnet-5-5` · coverage: **answered**

In the 10-K filed 2024-11-01, Apple only noted that U.S.-China tensions had in the past led to tariffs and restrictions, and that further escalation could materially adversely affect its business. Starting with the 10-Q filed 2025-05-02, it described new U.S. tariffs announced from the second quarter of 2025, retaliatory tariffs by other countries, and a Section 232 semiconductor investigation, warning of material adverse impacts on its supply chain, pricing and gross margin. By the 10-Q filed 2025-08-01, it reported that tariffs partially offset gross margin gains. By 2026 it said the January 2026 Section 232 initial results imposed no additional tariffs on its products, and that after a February 20, 2026 Supreme Court ruling it applied for refunds of tariffs paid. The 10-Q filed 2026-07-31 also mentions recent Section 301 tariffs and says tariff refunds helped raise products gross margin.
