# What has Apple said about tariffs over the last two years?

AAPL · 12 passages · model `claude-sonnet-5-5` · coverage: **answered**

In the 10-K filed 2024-11-01, tariffs were mentioned only generally as having resulted from past U.S.-China tensions. From the 10-Q filed 2025-05-02, the filings described new U.S. tariffs announced from the second quarter of 2025 on imports from China, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam and the EU, along with reciprocal or retaliatory measures. The company said tariffs can materially adversely affect its business, supply chain, pricing and gross margin, and that the ultimate impact remains uncertain. The 10-Q filed 2025-08-01 said products gross margin rose despite being partially offset by tariffs. By the 10-Q filed 2026-01-30, the filings noted initial Section 232 semiconductor investigation results that imposed no additional tariffs on its products, and the 10-Q filed 2026-07-31 reported a Supreme Court ruling striking down certain tariffs, a refund application, and recent Section 301 tariffs.
