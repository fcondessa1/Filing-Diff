Apple has stated that tariffs and other measures applied to its products or components can have a material adverse impact on its business, results of operations, and financial condition, including effects on supply chain, raw materials, pricing, and gross margin. In January 2026, initial results of a Section 232 investigation into semiconductor imports were published without imposing additional tariffs affecting Apple's products at that time. In February 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court struck down certain tariffs previously imposed, and Apple applied for refunds of tariffs paid and recognized received refunds as a reduction in product cost of sales. Apple has emphasized that the ultimate impact of tariffs remains uncertain and depends on whether additional tariffs are announced, the extent of retaliatory measures by other countries, and the overall magnitude and duration of these measures.
