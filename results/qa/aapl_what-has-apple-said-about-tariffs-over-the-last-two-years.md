Apple noted that tariffs and other measures applied to its products or components could have material adverse impacts on its business, supply chain, availability of raw materials, pricing, and gross margin. By January 2026, initial results of a Section 232 investigation into semiconductor imports were published but did not impose additional tariffs affecting Apple's products at that time.
