# Summarize What has Apple said about tariffs over the last two years?

Beginning in the second quarter of 2025, new U.S. tariffs were announced on imports from China, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam and the EU, with several countries imposing or threatening reciprocal tariffs in response. Apple has stated that tariffs and other measures applied to its products or components can have a material adverse impact on its business, results of operations, financial condition, supply chain, availability of raw materials and components, pricing and gross margin. The company has noted that various modifications to U.S. tariffs have been announced and further changes could be made, which may include additional sector-based tariffs or other measures under Section 232 semiconductor investigation. By January 2026, the Section 232 semiconductor investigation initial results did not impose additional tariffs affecting Apple's products, and by February 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court struck down certain tariffs previously imposed, with Apple applying for refunds of tariffs paid. As of July 2026, Apple reported that new tariffs under Section 301 of the Trade Act had been imposed, and the company recognized tariff refunds received as a reduction of product cost of sales.
